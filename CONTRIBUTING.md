# Contributing

Contributions are welcome: bug reports, fixes, features, and new templates. See the
[README](README.md#building-from-source) for how to build the app.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before your first pull request can be merged, you need to sign the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA bot will comment on your pull request with
instructions. You sign by replying with this exact comment:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

You only need to sign once; the signature covers all your future pull requests. You keep the
copyright in your work. For code, the CLA gives the maintainer, and anyone the project is
later transferred to (for example a company that acquires it), the right to relicense and
commercialize your contribution, including under proprietary licenses. Versions already
released under the AGPL stay available under it. Artwork
(illustrations, icons, templates) is different: it stays yours, and it is only ever shared
under the license you choose.

## Licensing

You keep the copyright in your work. Code you contribute is licensed under
the [GNU AGPL v3.0 or later](LICENSE), the same license as the rest of the repository. Artwork
(illustrations, icons, templates) stays under the license you choose for it, CC0 or CC BY 4.0;
see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
Credit is shown under whatever name you give, and a pseudonym is fine. If you'd rather not be
credited, submit artwork under CC0 and leave the credit name blank.

## Name and logo

Contributing doesn't give you rights to the BioCartoon name or logo. If you publish a
modified version, give it a different name and logo; see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

## Third-party assets

If a pull request adds code, images, or icons you didn't create, say where they come from and
under what license. Assets must allow redistribution and modification, including commercial
use (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, MIT, BSD, public domain, or similar; no NC or ND licenses), and their
license and credit go in the asset database.
